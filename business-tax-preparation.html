Business Tax Preparation in Brea, CA

We prepare tax returns for business owners and organizations, including LLCs, partnerships, S-Corps, C-Corps, and nonprofits. Each return is reviewed in multiple stages under Eunsang Lee, EA, an Enrolled Agent enrolled to practice before the IRS. Service is available in Korean and English, for businesses in Brea and nearby cities and remotely.

What we handle

  • Partnership returns (Form 1065)
  • S-Corporation returns (Form 1120-S)
  • C-Corporation returns (Form 1120)
  • LLC returns, filed according to how the LLC is taxed
  • Nonprofit returns (Form 990 and 990-PF)
  • Multi-state business returns
  • Amended returns
  • Foreign asset reporting (FBAR and FATCA) for owners with overseas accounts
  • Coordination with your bookkeeping and payroll so the numbers behind the return are ready

How it works

  1. 1

Frequently Asked Questions

Which form does my LLC file?

It depends on how the LLC is taxed, such as a partnership, S-Corp, or C-Corp. We confirm this with you at the start.

Can you work with my bookkeeper or payroll provider?

Yes. We coordinate with your existing bookkeeping and payroll so that the records behind the return are consistent. We can also discuss accounting support with you.

Do you prepare returns for nonprofits and foundations?

Yes. We prepare Form 990 and 990-PF returns.

My business operates in more than one state. Can you help?

Yes. We prepare multi-state business returns and review the states involved with you.
