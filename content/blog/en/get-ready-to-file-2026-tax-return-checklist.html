You will file your 2026 return in spring 2027, but it helps to start preparing now. On its "Get ready to file your taxes" page, the IRS lists what to do ahead of time: gather documents, set up an IRS online account, and confirm the account where your refund will go. Here is a summary.

IRS Announcement

“The fastest and most secure way for you to get your tax refund is by filing electronically and choosing direct deposit.”IRS, Get ready to file your taxes · Read the original

Key Points

  • IRS online account: Create one at IRS.gov/account or use the IRS app. You can view key details from returns filed in the last five years (including adjusted gross income) and transcripts, information returns such as W-2s and certain 1099s, your tax balance and payments, payment plans, and refund status.
  • Documents to gather: W-2s from employers; 1099s for unemployment, dividends, retirement distributions, gig work, interest and digital assets; Form 1095-A for Marketplace coverage and Premium Tax Credits; bank routing and account numbers for direct deposit; and IRS letters, including the CP01A notice for a new IP PIN.
  • IP PIN: A six-digit number that prevents someone else from filing a federal return using your SSN or ITIN. The IRS recommends signing up through your online account. If you cannot create one, in-person authentication at a Taxpayer Assistance Center is an alternative.
  • Direct deposit: The IRS describes electronic filing with direct deposit as the fastest and most secure way to get a refund. Enter routing and account numbers correctly.
  • ITIN expiration: An ITIN not included on a return for tax years 2023, 2024 and 2025 expires on December 31, 2026.
  • Refund timing: Refunds with EITC or the Additional Child Tax Credit cannot be issued before mid-February under PATH Act rules, and the IRS suggests checking "Where's My Refund" in late February. Most refunds are issued in under 21 days, but the IRS advises against relying on a specific date.
  • Estimated tax: There are four payment periods, and the last payment is due in mid-January.
  • Free filing options: IRS Free File, Free File Fillable Forms, MilTax for service members and some veterans, and VITA and TCE (no-cost basic preparation for qualified individuals).

Eunsang Lee, EA: What This Means for You

  • This IRS page gives no specific mailing dates for W-2s and 1099s. They generally arrive early in the year, but timing can differ by document. Filing before all documents arrive can mean an amended return later, so check that nothing is missing first.
  • The list of information returns in your IRS online account can help you check that no document was overlooked. But what you see there may not include every document, so compare it with what you received by mail or email.
  • If someone in your family uses an ITIN, check the expiration condition. Whether renewal is needed differs by person, so confirm individually.
  • If you receive an IP PIN, enter it accurately on your return, and keep the IRS letter (CP01A). How to enter it and how often it is renewed need individual confirmation on the IRS IP PIN page.
  • No one can promise a refund date. Avoid planning large early-year spending around an expected refund.
  • Korean bank accounts, foreign income, and child or family documents may require paperwork that is not on a general list. Whether that applies to you needs individual confirmation.
