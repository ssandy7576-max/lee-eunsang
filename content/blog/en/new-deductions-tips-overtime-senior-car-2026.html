Four new federal deductions apply from 2025 through 2028: tips, overtime pay, interest on a new car loan, and an extra deduction for people 65 and older. You can claim them without itemizing, but the conditions are strict and easy to miss or get wrong. Here is what to check now, before you file for 2026.

IRS Announcement

“The $6,000 senior deduction is per eligible individual”IRS, Working Families Tax Cuts: Tax deductions for working Americans and seniors · Read the original

Key Points

  • Tips: Up to $25,000, for occupations that customarily receive tips. The deduction phases down when modified AGI exceeds $150,000 ($300,000 married filing jointly).
  • Overtime: Up to $12,500 ($25,000 joint). Only the premium portion counts, the extra "half" of time-and-a-half, not the full overtime pay. Same income limits as tips.
  • Car loan interest: Up to $10,000 a year. The loan must be taken out after 2024 for a new, personal-use vehicle with final assembly in the United States. Leases do not qualify, and the VIN must be listed on your return. Phases down above $100,000 ($200,000 joint).
  • Age 65 and older: $6,000 per eligible person ($12,000 if both spouses qualify), on top of the existing additional standard deduction for seniors. You must be 65 by December 31 of the tax year. Phases down above $75,000 ($150,000 joint).
  • Common rules: Available whether you itemize or not (Schedule 1-A). A Social Security number valid for work is required, and married couples must file jointly.
  • 2026 Form W-2: Starting with 2026 W-2s, box 12 shows qualified overtime (code TT) and tips (code TP) separately.

Eunsang Lee, EA: What This Means for You

  • The overtime deduction is not your total overtime pay. If you earn $30 an hour and $45 for overtime hours, only the extra $15 counts. Only overtime required by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act qualifies, so California daily overtime (over 8 hours in a day) may not count. Confirm for your situation.
  • Check your December pay stub to see whether overtime and tips are shown separately, and check box 12 codes TT and TP on the W-2 you receive in January. If they are missing or wrong, ask your employer for a correction.
  • Planning to buy a car before year-end? Check the rules before you sign. Used cars, leases, cash purchases and business vehicles do not qualify. You can check final assembly on the dealer's vehicle label or by VIN.
  • The senior deduction shrinks as income rises. If you plan a large IRA withdrawal this year, run the numbers on timing and amount first.
  • Married couples filing separately cannot take any of the four deductions. If you are considering separate returns, include this in the comparison.
  • These are federal deductions. California follows its own rules, so check the state impact separately.
