Frequently Asked Questions

I just received an IRS letter. What should I do first?

Do not ignore it. Note the response date on the letter and send us a copy through the secure portal so we can review it before the deadline.

Can you speak with the IRS for me?

Yes. With your signed authorization (Form 2848), we can communicate with the IRS on your behalf as your Enrolled Agent.

Can my penalties be removed?

It depends on your filing history and the reason for the penalty. We review whether First-Time Abatement or reasonable cause may apply.

I live in Fullerton, Anaheim, or Irvine. Can you still help?

Yes. Most IRS matters are handled by phone, mail, and the secure portal, so you do not need to visit the office.
