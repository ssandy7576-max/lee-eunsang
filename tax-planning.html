Frequently Asked Questions

Is tax planning only for high-income clients?

No. Wage earners, families, and small business owners can also benefit from reviewing withholding, retirement contributions, and credits before the year ends.

When should we start?

Ideally before year-end, while there is still time to act. Some steps must be in place by December 31 to count for that year.

Can you review returns I already filed?

Yes. We review the prior three years for missed deductions and credits. Whether an amended return is appropriate depends on the facts of each case.

I live in Irvine or Anaheim, not Brea. Can we still work together?

Yes. Meetings can be in person or remote, and documents move through the secure portal.
