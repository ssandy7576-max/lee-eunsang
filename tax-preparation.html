Frequently Asked Questions

Can you prepare my return if I live outside Brea?

Yes. Our service is paperless and remote, so you can work with us from anywhere.

I lived or worked in more than one state. Can you help?

Yes. We prepare multi-state returns and will review which states are involved once we see your documents.

Do you handle foreign bank or asset reporting?

Yes. We assist with FBAR and FATCA reporting. Whether a filing is required depends on your specific situation, which we review with you.

Can you look at returns I already filed?

Yes. We can review prior returns to see whether an amended return may be appropriate. Eligibility depends on the facts of each case.
